02 · concept

What Marginalia is.

locked

The pitch concept in its locked form. This is the version that the deck mockups and zine brief have been built against. Do not relitigate.

a creator-in-residence proposal — for Book of the Month
Marginalia.

A monthly collage-essay on the interior life of reading.

"The reading life of any woman, anywhere — filmed and written with craft."

format 6–9 min film + companion printed essay-object + 3 short cuts cadence monthly sample episode Lost Lambs by Madeline Cash · BOTM Jan 2026 publishing register Volume-Ø adjacent
voice anchor — the balcony paragraph
The source paragraph for Field 3 drafting. Mia's voice. Preserve verbatim or paraphrase carefully.

Sitting out on a balcony in Paris, watching the sitting reading and thinking and drinking coffee and smoking and writing in your journal while listening to music and perhaps talking passionately with a friend about the book you're reading and how it makes you feel — what you see vs. what they see, the poetry and prose it inspires in you, the change it inspires, how it touches your own life, how you see the world when you come out of the world within the book, how it immediately becomes nostalgic and fuzzy because you can never go back to the feeling of reading the book for the first time, how it feels to hold a book close to your heart, your mind, for it to inspire deep thought, feeling, and discussion, how it connects to the world.

core characterization moves
Three modalities of the reader-figure across the work.
  • cinematic bodycomposed, framed, intentional. the held shot. the deliberate gesture.
  • casual / webcam bodyunguarded, low-light, intimate. the body present without performance.
  • written word as text on screenthe writing made visible. fragments appearing over image, voice always present.
register · what to do and what to avoid
Tonal guardrails for every artifact.
  • doglobal, not European. art-essay register. intellectually serious, humanly warm. quiet, considered, with breath.
  • do notcozy, me-time, lifestyle-coded, Kinfolk-perfect, lifestyle-flatlay aesthetic. nothing gendered as feminine-domestic.
  • doname authors and books specifically. say "reader" not "user." say "work" not "content." cut adverbs.
  • do notpitch the whole catalog. tied to BOTM's literary fiction / Volume 0 lane only.
03 · strategic context

Why this pitch, why now, why this lane.

research summary

Background context that informs every artifact in the sprint. BOTM is at a strategic moment — institution-building around its centennial — and Marginalia positions as the content arm of that moment.

01 · institution context

BOTM at 100.

Book of the Month turns 100 in 2026. Four institutional moves visible right now — the Lolly Award ($25K fiction prize), Volume 0 (literary short-fiction magazine), Aspen Literary Festival presenting sponsorship, and the Creator-in-Residence Program. The pitch positions Marginalia as content in service of this institution-building moment.

02 · the unlock context

Volume 0 alignment.

Volume 0 is BOTM's literary short-fiction magazine — Emma Cline, Nada Alic, Kristen Roupenian on the roster, $35K author fees, CEO John Lippman edits. The companion printed essay-object positions Marginalia as Volume-Ø adjacent in publishing register. This is the strategic key — converts a creator gig into a publishing relationship.

03 · audience context

The literary lane.

Pitch is for the literary subset of BOTM's base — readers of Madeline Cash, Kiran Millwood Hargrave, Aja Gabel, Erin Somers, Emily Adrian, Beatriz Serrano, Emma Cline. Not the romance / commercial fiction audience. The deck mockups and zine brief are calibrated to this taste cluster.

voice + tone — Mia's preferences
How to talk to Mia, what register to write in.

Direct, considered, intellectually serious. No corporate gloss, no motivational language, no performed enthusiasm. Default to verbose and detailed — more information is better than less. Don't bullet-point your way out of real explanation.

Mia's reference points: philosophy, the arts, music, theology (academic, never devotional), spiritual theory, creative direction, copywriting, art direction, creative technology, filmmaking, production. When pulling from these, go deep — lineages of thought, primary sources, adjacent frameworks.

For practical work: smallest actionable units. Reduce friction. Don't comment on the friction. The gap between conception and completion is the central problem — help Mia define the smallest completable version, not the ideal version.
